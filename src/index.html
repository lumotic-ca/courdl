Set up CourDL

Import cookies from a logged-in Coursera session (Netscape or Cookie-Editor JSON). CAUTH is required. Cookies are login-equivalent and usually expire in about two weeks. Lectures, subtitles, and readings download to disk. Quizzes and graded work stay on Coursera.
